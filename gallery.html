HAWA ABDULKADIR SHEGO

Director – Head of Business | HAWRAJ GLOBAL INDUSTRIES LTD.

As Director and Head of Business at HAWRAJ GLOBAL INDUSTRIES LTD., she leads the company's direction, growth and day-to-day business strategy. She brings a hands-on, results-focused approach to leadership, with a firm commitment to professionalism, integrity and customer satisfaction.

She oversees the company's business operations, guides its service offering across industrial, engineering, construction and general supply solutions, and builds the trusted client and partner relationships on which the company is founded.

Under her leadership, HAWRAJ GLOBAL INDUSTRIES LTD. continues to work towards its vision of becoming a trusted leader in industrial and engineering solutions in Kenya and across East Africa.

Key Areas of Responsibility

  • Corporate Leadership & Strategic Direction
  • Business Development & Growth
  • Client & Partner Relationships
  • Operations & Quality Standards
  • Project Oversight & Delivery
  • Company Governance & Compliance
Message from the Director: We are committed to delivering quality, reliability and integrity in everything we do, and to building lasting partnerships with every client we serve.

Mr. Ashutosh Raj Singh

Business Head – India | HAWRAJ GLOBAL INDUSTRIES LTD.

Mr. Ashutosh Raj Singh is an integral part of HAWRAJ GLOBAL INDUSTRIES LTD., serving as Business Head – India. Originally from India, he brings a business-oriented approach, a strong understanding of the Indian business environment, and a commitment to developing strategic business opportunities across international markets.

He is responsible for driving business development initiatives, identifying new market opportunities, building strategic partnerships, coordinating with manufacturers and suppliers, and strengthening the company's business network between India, Africa and other international markets.

With a focus on professionalism, innovation, trust and long-term business relationships, Mr. Singh plays an important role in advancing the company's vision of becoming a reliable global business partner.

Key Areas of Responsibility

  • Business Development & Market Expansion
  • India–Africa Trade & Business Relations
  • Strategic Partnerships & Corporate Alliances
  • Industrial Procurement & Supply Chain Coordination
  • Manufacturer & Supplier Networking
  • International Business Opportunities
Our Vision: To build strong international partnerships and create sustainable business opportunities through trust, commitment and excellence.
